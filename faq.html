Pricing

Are the Pro features available today?

Not yet. Ticket reading (OCR), the dispatch board, driver settlements, and QuickBooks-style export are Coming soon, and no launch date is listed. Pro is not sold until those features ship. Everything available today is included in Core.

Are there setup fees, contracts, or per-user charges?

No. There is no setup fee and no contract. Core and Pro include unlimited users, so owners, dispatchers, office staff, and drivers do not add cost. The price is set by the number of registered trucks.

How is a fleet with 26 or more trucks priced?

Fleets with 26 or more trucks are priced by quote on the Fleet plan. Book a demo to get a Fleet quote.

How much do I save by paying annually?

Annual billing is 20% off the monthly price: Core is $12 per truck per month and Pro is $20 per truck per month when billed annually.

How much does RouteRock cost?

RouteRock is priced per registered truck per month. Free covers 1 truck and 1 driver. Core is $15 per truck per month, or $12 per truck per month billed annually. Pro is $25 per truck per month, or $20 per truck per month billed annually. Fleets with 26 or more trucks get a Fleet quote. Prices are in US dollars.

Yes. The Free plan covers 1 truck and 1 driver. It includes the time clock, ticket photos, ticket history, missing-ticket alerts, an offline queue for clock events, and the company portal for trucks and sub-haulers. GPS is not included on Free.

What counts as a truck?

What is a load cycle in dump truck hauling?

A load cycle in dump truck hauling is one trip that moves material. The truck loads, hauls, dumps, and returns for the next load. The cycle usually produces a ticket the office uses for the haul. In RouteRock today, the driver photographs that ticket, or picks one from the gallery, while on shift. What is geofencing in dump truck hauling?

Geofencing in dump truck hauling is a boundary around a load site or a dump site. When a truck's location enters or leaves that boundary, the haul can be marked as on site or off site.
