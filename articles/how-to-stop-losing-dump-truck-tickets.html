Dump tickets

How to stop losing dump truck tickets

October 22, 2026

A dump ticket that never returns to the office leaves the haul without a record.

Where do paper dump tickets go?

They sit in a cupholder, a door pocket, or on the dash. Rain makes them wet. A damaged slip is hard to read. Once it is gone, the haul has no verifiable record.

The photo path is on the dump truck ticketing app page.

What does a photo change?

The driver photographs the ticket and can open it full screen. Submitted tickets stay in a list, and missing ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a photo. The company can see the photo.

The same steps are on haul ticket tracking.

What about the pile at month end?

Reconciling missing tickets at month end is the same gap, deferred. The submitted list and the missing-ticket alerts show which hauls still need a photo. Invoicing and accounts receivable are coming soon.
