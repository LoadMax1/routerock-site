Paper tickets vs digital ticketing for dump trucks

October 22, 2026

The difference is what is still there when the truck gets back.

What goes wrong with a paper ticket?

A paper ticket gets lost, wet, or crumpled. It does not record when the driver clocked in. At month end someone is still chasing the slip.

What is a digital ticket in RouteRock?

The driver photographs the ticket and can open it full screen. Clock-in and clock-out times fill in automatically. Drivers cannot edit those times. Submitted tickets are listed, and the app includes missing-ticket alerts.

See it on the dump truck ticketing app page.

Does digital ticketing mean dispatch?

Not yet. Dump truck dispatch software is not what the beta is. The owner live map, invoicing and reports, and admin screens are coming soon. Pricing is not set yet.

The note on price is on RouteRock pricing.
