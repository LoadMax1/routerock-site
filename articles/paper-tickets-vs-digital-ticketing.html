Paper tickets vs digital ticketing for dump trucks

October 22, 2026

The difference is what is still there when the truck gets back.

What goes wrong with a paper ticket?

A paper ticket gets lost, wet, or damaged. It does not record when the driver clocked in. At month end, teams still hunt for missing paper slips.

What is a digital ticket in RouteRock?

The driver photographs the ticket and can open it full screen. Clock-in and clock-out times fill in on their own, and drivers cannot edit them. Submitted tickets stay in a list, and missing ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a photo.

See it on the dump truck ticketing app page.

Does digital ticketing mean dispatch?

Not yet. Dispatch and a live fleet map are coming soon, along with invoicing and accounts receivable, payroll and settlements, and operations reporting. Arrival times are not included. Pricing is set with each fleet.

The note on price is on RouteRock pricing.
