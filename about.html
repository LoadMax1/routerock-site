About

Jerjuan Edwards is the founder of RouteRock.

The first customer is LoadMax Logistics in Charlotte, North Carolina. LoadMax hauls in North Carolina and South Carolina.

RouteRock is a mobile operations app for construction hauling companies. It is in beta and is planned so more than one company can use it. The name in the app stores is RouteRock.
