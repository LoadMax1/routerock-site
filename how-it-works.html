Haul ticket tracking: clock-in, ticket photo, and office visibility

The driver clocks in, photographs the ticket, and the company can see that record.

  1. 1

    Trucking time clock app

    The driver clocks in and the time fills in on its own. Drivers cannot edit it, and clock-out works the same way. GPS runs only while the driver is clocked in, in the foreground and in the background.

  2. 2

    How does the driver capture the ticket photo?

    The driver photographs the ticket, or picks one from the gallery, and can open it full screen. Submitted tickets stay in a list, and missing-ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a photo.

  3. 3

    Does it work without cell signal?

    If the phone is offline, clock events and GPS points stay in a queue and send when a connection is available. Ticket photos are not held in that queue.

  4. 4

    What can the company see?

    Owners and dispatchers see clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was on shift. Companies manage the truck roster and sub-haulers at app.routerockhub.com.

Is this dump truck dispatch software?

Not yet. Dispatch and a live fleet map are coming soon, including a dispatch board and live status on active hauls. Arrival times are not included. Today the company sees clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was on shift.
