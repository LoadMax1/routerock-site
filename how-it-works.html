Beta

Haul ticket tracking, from the photo to the office.

The driver clocks in, photographs the ticket, and the company can see that record.

Ticket details are on the dump truck ticketing app page. The company behind it is in Charlotte, NC.

Book a demo

  1. 1

    Trucking time clock app

    The driver clocks in. The time is filled in automatically, and the driver cannot change it. While clocked in, the app tracks precise location in the foreground and in the background. Clock out is the same: the time fills in, and it cannot be edited.

  2. 2

    How does the driver snap the ticket?

    A photo of the ticket can be opened full screen. Submitted tickets are listed in the app. Missing-ticket alerts are included, for the ticket that would otherwise slow down getting paid.

  3. 3

    Does it work without cell signal?

    If the device is offline, clock events and GPS points stay in a queue and send when a connection is available. You are not waiting on a bar of service to keep the record.

  4. 4

    Is this dump truck dispatch software?

    Not yet. The company can see clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was clocked in. An owner live map, invoicing and reports, and admin screens are coming soon.
