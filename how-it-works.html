How it works

Drivers use the app on the job. The driver's company can see the clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was clocked in.

Driver

  1. Sign in with driver login.
  2. Clock in. The time is filled in automatically. Drivers cannot change it.
  3. While clocked in, the app tracks GPS, including in the background.
  4. Photograph a ticket, open it full screen, and see it on the submitted tickets list.
  5. Missing-ticket alerts are in the app.
  6. If the device is offline, clock events and GPS points stay in a queue and send when a connection is available.
  7. Settings and feedback are in the app.
  8. Clock out. The clock-out time is filled in automatically. Drivers cannot change it.

Owner and dispatcher

The driver's company can see the data the app collects for that driver's work. That includes clock-in and clock-out times, ticket photos, and precise location from while the driver was clocked in.
