Where was this dump truck ticketing app built?

RouteRock was built inside a working dump-truck company in the Charlotte, NC area.

It works for dump-truck fleets nationwide, in all 50 states. RouteRock is in early access. It runs on iPhone and Android and is not a public App Store or Google Play release.

The same notes are on About.

What does the app include?

Available now: driver time tracking with auto-captured clock-in and clock-out times that drivers cannot edit; digital load tickets photographed or picked from the camera or gallery while on shift; submitted ticket photos the company and the driver can open full screen; missing-ticket alerts after the threshold; GPS only during the shift; an offline queue for clock events and GPS points; company visibility for owners and dispatchers; and a company portal where companies manage trucks and sub-haulers at app.routerockhub.com. Ticket photos are not queued offline.

Details are on the dump truck ticketing app page and on haul ticket tracking.

What is still coming soon?

Coming soon: dispatch and a live fleet map, ticket OCR, geofencing and route history, invoicing and accounts receivable, payroll and settlements, profit and operations reporting, and a notification center. Arrival times are not included. Dump truck dispatch software is not available yet. No launch date is listed.

Price is covered on RouteRock pricing.

How do I book a demo?

A demo is about 15 minutes. It covers the driver flow and what the company can see today, including what is available now and what is coming soon. Book a demo.
