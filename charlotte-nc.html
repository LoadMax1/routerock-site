Where was this dump truck ticketing app built?

RouteRock was built inside LoadMax Logistics, a working hauling company in Charlotte, North Carolina. LoadMax hauls in North Carolina and South Carolina. The first customer is LoadMax Logistics. The app is in beta and is planned so more than one company can use it. The name in the app stores is RouteRock.

What does the beta include?

Drivers sign in, clock in and out with times they cannot edit, photograph a ticket, and see a submitted list. Missing-ticket alerts are included. Clock events and GPS points can wait offline and send when a connection is available. Precise location runs only while the driver is clocked in.

Details are on the dump truck ticketing app page and on haul ticket tracking.

What is still coming soon?

The owner live map, invoicing and reports, and admin screens are coming soon. Dump truck dispatch software is not what the beta is. Pricing is not set yet.

Price is covered on RouteRock pricing.
