What does the demo cover?

Plan on about 15 minutes.

Driver flow

Sign in, clock in and out with auto-captured times the driver cannot edit, and photograph or pick a dump ticket while on shift. Submitted ticket photos open full screen, and missing-ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a photo. The offline queue holds clock events and GPS points. Ticket photos are not queued offline. GPS runs only while the driver is clocked in. The same path is on how a haul gets on record.

Owner flow

What the company can see today: clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was on shift. Companies also manage trucks and sub-haulers in the company portal. The demo distinguishes available features from items marked Beta or Coming soon.

Beta and coming soon

A live map, geofencing and route history, accounts receivable, driver settlements, profit and operations reporting, and a notification center are coming soon. A dispatch board, invoicing, driver payroll, and tie-out reports are in beta with a small group of early-access fleets, and ticket OCR is Beta. Arrival times are not included. Beta and Coming soon items are labeled and are not shown as available now. Notes are on the dump truck ticketing app page.

Early access

RouteRock is in early access. Plans and per-truck prices are on RouteRock pricing, starting with a free plan for 1 truck. Book a demo to get started or to request a Fleet quote for 26 or more trucks. For help with an account, use Status and support. Account deletion is on Delete account.
