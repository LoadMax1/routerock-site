What does the demo cover?

Plan on about 15 minutes.

Driver flow

Sign in, clock in and out with times that fill in on their own, a ticket photo and full-screen view, the submitted list, and missing ticket alerts. The offline queue holds clock events and GPS points, not ticket photos. GPS runs only while the driver is clocked in. The same path is on how a haul gets on record.

Owner flow

What the company can see today: clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was clocked in. We will say plainly what is not available yet.

Coming soon

The owner live map, invoicing and reports, and admin screens are coming soon. We will not pretend they are in the demo. Notes are on the dump truck ticketing app page.
