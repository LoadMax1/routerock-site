What does the demo cover?

Plan on about 15 minutes.

Driver flow

Sign in, clock in and out with times the driver cannot edit, a ticket photo and full-screen view, the submitted list, missing-ticket alerts, and the offline queue. GPS runs only while the driver is clocked in. The same path is on how a haul gets on record.

Owner flow

What the company can see today: clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was clocked in. We will say plainly what is not in the beta.

Not in the beta

The owner live map, invoicing and reports, and admin screens are coming soon. We will not pretend they are in the demo. Notes are on the dump truck ticketing app page.

How do I request beta access?

Pricing is not set yet. See RouteRock pricing, then ask to join the beta with the same address. For help with an account, use Support. Account deletion is on Delete account.
