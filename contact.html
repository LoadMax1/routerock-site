What does the demo cover?

Plan on about 15 minutes.

Driver flow

Sign in, clock in and out with auto-captured times the driver cannot edit, and photograph or pick a dump ticket while on shift. Submitted ticket photos open full screen, and missing-ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a photo. The offline queue holds clock events and GPS points. Ticket photos are not queued offline. GPS runs only while the driver is clocked in. The same path is on how a haul gets on record.

Owner flow

What the company can see today: clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was on shift. Companies also manage trucks and sub-haulers in the company portal. The demo distinguishes available features from items marked Coming soon.

Coming soon

Dispatch and a live map, ticket OCR, geofencing and route history, invoicing and accounts receivable, payroll and settlements, profit and operations reporting, and a notification center are coming soon. Arrival times are not included. Coming-soon items are labeled and are not shown as available. Notes are on the dump truck ticketing app page.

Early access

RouteRock is in early access. Pricing is set per fleet. Book a demo to discuss pricing and early access. For help with an account, use Support. Account deletion is on Delete account.
