Service status and how to get help

Fleets and drivers can check the current RouteRock status on this page and email support when something needs a look. Product questions are on the FAQ.

No known issues

Get help

Email support@routerockhub.com. If the question is about a driver account, include that account email.

How to report a problem

In that email, include:

  • Company name
  • The driver or the truck
  • What happened
  • The time it happened
  • Screenshots, if you have them

What the app does offline

Clock events and GPS points queue on the phone and send when a connection is available.

To delete an account, follow Delete account.

Questions

How do I report a problem with RouteRock?

Email support@routerockhub.com. Include the company name, the driver or the truck, what happened, the time it happened, and screenshots if you have them.

How do I check RouteRock service status?

The Current status note on this page is updated by hand and shows the date it was last updated. It is not a live uptime feed.

Does the RouteRock app work offline?

Clock events and GPS points queue on the phone and send when a connection is available.

How do I book a RouteRock demo?

Book a demo on the contact page. A demo is about 15 minutes and covers the driver flow and what the company can see.

How do I delete a RouteRock account?

From the account email, write to support@routerockhub.com with the subject line Delete my RouteRock account. Deletion is completed within 30 days. The steps are on the Delete account page.
