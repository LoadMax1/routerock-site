Support

For help with RouteRock, email loadmaxlog@gmail.com. If the question is about a driver account, include that account email.

Common questions

What is RouteRock?

RouteRock is a mobile operations app for construction hauling companies. Drivers use it on the job. The app is in beta. The name in the app stores is RouteRock.

In the beta, drivers can sign in, clock in and out, upload ticket photos and open them full screen, see a list of submitted tickets, get missing-ticket alerts, and send feedback. Settings are in the app. Clock events and GPS points can wait in an offline queue. The app tracks GPS while a driver is clocked in, including background tracking.

Who is it for?

Owners and dispatchers at dump-truck companies, and the drivers who work for them. Jerjuan Edwards is the founder. The first company using RouteRock is LoadMax Logistics in Charlotte, North Carolina. LoadMax hauls in North Carolina and South Carolina. RouteRock is planned so more than one company can use it.

Can a driver change a clock time?

Clock-in and clock-out times are filled in automatically. Drivers cannot change them.

How do tickets work?

A driver uploads a photo of a ticket and can open it in a full-screen viewer. Submitted tickets are listed in the app. The app includes missing-ticket alerts.

What does the offline queue do?

The app keeps an offline queue for clock events and GPS points. Those items stay on the device and send when a connection is available.

When does location tracking run?

Precise location is collected in the foreground and in the background only while a driver is clocked in. It is stored in our Supabase database and is visible to the driver’s company. Read the privacy policy for the full account of what the app collects.

Where are settings and feedback?

Both are in the app. Feedback is sent from inside RouteRock.

What is coming soon?

An owner live map, invoicing and reports, and admin screens are coming soon. They are not in the beta.

How do I delete an account?

Email loadmaxlog@gmail.com from the account email with the subject Delete my RouteRock account. We delete the account and its data within 30 days. The company may retain records it is legally required to keep. Steps are on the Delete account page.

Who do I contact about privacy?

Email loadmaxlog@gmail.com. The policy is on the Privacy page. RouteRock is operated by LoadMax Logistics, Charlotte, NC.
