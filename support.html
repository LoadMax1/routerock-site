Common questions

What is RouteRock?

RouteRock is a mobile operations app for construction hauling companies, in early access. Drivers clock in and out with auto-captured times they cannot edit, photograph or pick a dump ticket while on shift, and open submitted ticket photos full screen. Missing-ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a ticket photo. Clock events and GPS points wait in an offline queue and sync when online. Ticket photos are not queued offline. GPS runs only while a driver is clocked in. Owners and dispatchers see clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was on shift. Companies manage trucks and sub-haulers in the company portal at app.routerockhub.com. RouteRock runs on iPhone and Android during early access. It is not a public App Store or Google Play release.

Who is it for?

Owners and dispatchers at dump-truck companies, and the drivers who work for them. RouteRock is for fleets in all 50 states. It was built inside a working dump-truck company in the Charlotte, NC area.

Can a driver change a clock time?

No. Clock-in and clock-out times fill in on their own. Drivers cannot change them.

How do tickets work?

The driver photographs the ticket, or picks one from the camera or gallery, while on shift and can open it full screen. Submitted tickets stay in a list, and missing-ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a photo after the threshold.

What does the offline queue do?

Clock events and GPS points stay in an offline queue on the phone and send when a connection is available. Ticket photos are not held in that queue.

When does location tracking run?

GPS runs only while the driver is clocked in, in the foreground and in the background. It is stored in our Supabase database and is visible to the driver’s company. Read the privacy policy for the full account of what the app collects.

Where are settings and feedback?

Both are in the app. Feedback is sent from inside RouteRock.

What is coming soon?

Dispatch and a live fleet map, ticket OCR, geofencing and route history, invoicing and accounts receivable, payroll and settlements, profit and operations reporting, and a notification center are coming soon. Arrival times are not included. No launch date is listed.

How do I delete an account?

Email support@routerockhub.com from the account email with the subject Delete my RouteRock account. We delete the account and its data within 30 days. The company may retain records it is legally required to keep. Steps are on the Delete account page.

Who do I contact about privacy?

Email support@routerockhub.com. The policy is on the Privacy page.
