RouteRock pricing questions

How much does RouteRock cost?

RouteRock is priced per registered truck per month. Free covers 1 truck and 1 driver. Core is $15 per truck per month, or $12 per truck per month billed annually. Pro is $25 per truck per month, or $20 per truck per month billed annually. Fleets with 26 or more trucks get a Fleet quote. Prices are in US dollars.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan covers 1 truck and 1 driver. It includes the time clock, ticket photos, ticket history, and missing-ticket alerts. GPS is not included on Free.

What counts as a truck?

A registered truck is a truck on your company's truck roster in the RouteRock company portal. Paid plans are billed for each registered truck.

Are there setup fees, contracts, or per-user charges?

No. There is no setup fee and no contract. Core and Pro include unlimited users, so owners, dispatchers, office staff, and drivers do not add cost. The price is set by the number of registered trucks.

How much do I save by paying annually?

Annual billing is 20% off the monthly price: Core is $12 per truck per month and Pro is $20 per truck per month when billed annually.

What is the founding fleet offer?

The first 10 fleets to sign up for a paid plan get 25% off for their first 12 months, and their price is locked for 24 months. On monthly billing that is $11.25 per truck per month for Core and $18.75 per truck per month for Pro. The founding offer does not combine with the 20% annual discount.

Are the Pro features available today?

Not yet. Ticket reading (OCR), the dispatch board, driver settlements, and QuickBooks-style export are Coming soon, and no launch date is listed. Pro is not sold until those features ship. Everything available today is included in Core.

What is included in Core today?

Core includes everything available now: driver time tracking with clock times drivers cannot edit, digital load tickets, ticket history, missing-ticket alerts, GPS while clocked in, an offline queue for clock events and GPS points, company visibility, and the company portal for trucks and sub-haulers. See features.

Does RouteRock work without cell signal?

Clock events and GPS points queue on the phone and sync when a connection is available. Ticket photos are not queued offline. A connection is needed to send a ticket photo. Read running tickets when the job site has no signal.

Is RouteRock an ELD? Do I need extra hardware?

RouteRock is not an ELD and does not do FMCSA electronic logging. It runs on the drivers' iPhone or Android phones, and GPS comes from the phone while the driver is clocked in. No in-cab hardware is included or required.

How is a fleet with 26 or more trucks priced?

Fleets with 26 or more trucks are priced by quote on the Fleet plan. Book a demo to get a Fleet quote.

How do I get started?

Book a demo. A demo is about 15 minutes. It covers the driver flow, what the company can see today, and which features are Coming soon. The request goes to demo@routerockhub.com.
