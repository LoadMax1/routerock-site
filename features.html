Can drivers edit their clock times?

No. The driver clocks in and the time is filled in automatically. Clock out works the same way. While the driver is clocked in, the app tracks precise location in the foreground and in the background. That is the trucking time clock in the beta. The full order is on How it works.

How do haulers stop losing paper tickets?

A driver photographs the ticket and can open that photo full screen. Submitted tickets are listed in the app, and missing-ticket alerts are included. The company can see the photo. That is the fix for the slip that gets lost or crumpled.

Does it work without cell signal?

Clock events and GPS points stay in an offline queue on the device. They send when a connection is available. Location is still collected only while the driver is clocked in.

Is this dump truck dispatch software?

Not yet. Dispatch screens are not in the beta. Today the driver's company can see clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was clocked in. An owner live map, invoicing and reports, and admin screens are coming soon.
