What is in the dump truck ticketing app?

Clock times stay put

Times are filled in automatically. Drivers cannot change them.

The ticket is a photo

Drivers upload a photo of a ticket and can open it full screen.

Submitted tickets, listed

Submitted tickets are listed in the app, so you are not hunting a glovebox.

Location while clocked in

Precise location runs in the foreground and in the background only while the driver is clocked in. The company can see it.

The queue holds the gap

Clock events and GPS points stay on the device and send when a connection is available.

Missing tickets get an alert

The app includes alerts for missing tickets, the ones that slow down getting paid.

Drivers also sign in, use settings, and send feedback from inside the app.

Can drivers edit their clock times?

No. The driver clocks in and the time is filled in automatically. Clock out works the same way. While the driver is clocked in, the app tracks precise location in the foreground and in the background. That is the trucking time clock in the beta. The full order is on haul ticket tracking.

How do haulers stop losing paper tickets?

A driver photographs the ticket and can open that photo full screen. Submitted tickets are listed in the app, and missing-ticket alerts are included. The company can see the photo.

Where are submitted tickets listed?

Submitted tickets are listed in the app, so you are not hunting a glovebox.

When does a missing ticket get an alert?

The app includes alerts for missing tickets, the ones that slow down getting paid.

Does it work without cell signal?

Clock events and GPS points stay in an offline queue on the device. They send when a connection is available. Location is still collected only while the driver is clocked in.

Is this dump truck dispatch software?

Not yet. Dispatch screens are not in the beta. Today the driver's company can see clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was clocked in. An owner live map, invoicing and reports, and admin screens are coming soon. See how a haul gets on record.
