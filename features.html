What is in the dump truck ticketing app?

Driver time tracking

Clock in and out with auto-captured times. Drivers cannot edit them.

Digital load tickets

Photograph or pick a dump ticket from the camera or gallery while on shift.

Ticket history

The company and the driver see submitted ticket photos and can open them full screen.

GPS while clocked in

Location runs only during the shift and stops at clock-out.

Offline queue for clock and GPS

Clock events and GPS points queue and sync when online. Ticket photos are not queued offline.

Missing-ticket alerts

Flags a haul that still needs a ticket photo after the threshold.

Company visibility

Owners and dispatchers see clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was on shift.

Company portal — trucks & sub-haulers

Companies manage the truck roster and sub-haulers at app.routerockhub.com.

Ticket photos are not queued offline. A connection is required to submit a ticket photo.

Can drivers edit their clock times?

No. Clock-in and clock-out times fill in on their own. Drivers cannot edit them, so the company keeps the time the driver started and stopped. GPS runs only while the driver is clocked in, in the foreground and in the background. The full order is on haul ticket tracking.

How do haulers stop losing paper tickets?

The driver photographs the ticket, or picks one from the gallery, and can open it full screen. The company sees the photo instead of a lost paper slip. Submitted tickets stay in a list, and missing-ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a photo.

Where are submitted tickets listed?

Submitted ticket photos stay in a list. The company and the driver can open a photo full screen.

When does a missing ticket get an alert?

Missing-ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a ticket photo after the threshold. The company can see which loads are missing one.

Does it work without cell signal?

Clock events and GPS points stay on the phone and sync when a connection is available. Ticket photos are not queued offline. GPS still runs only while the driver is clocked in.

Is this dump truck dispatch software?

Not yet. Today the company can see clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was on shift. Dispatch is coming soon and does not include arrival times. See how a haul gets on record.
