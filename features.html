Beta

Features

These features are in the current beta. Drivers sign in and use them on the job.

Driver login

Drivers sign in to an account.

Clock in and clock out

Times are filled in automatically. Drivers cannot change them.

Ticket photos

Drivers upload a photo of a ticket and can open it full screen.

Submitted tickets

Submitted tickets are listed in the app.

Offline queue

Clock events and GPS points stay in a queue on the device and send when a connection is available.

Missing-ticket alerts

The app includes alerts for missing tickets.

Settings

Settings are in the app.

In-app feedback

Feedback can be sent from inside the app.

GPS while clocked in

The app tracks location while a driver is clocked in, including background tracking. Precise location is collected in the foreground and in the background only while the driver is clocked in, and it is visible to the driver's company. The privacy policy describes what is stored.
