Beta

Clock times, tickets, and location for dump-truck work.

RouteRock is a mobile operations app for construction hauling companies. Drivers clock in, photograph tickets, and share location while they are clocked in.

Request a demo See features

What dispatch needs

The work to track

Owners and dispatchers need the clock time, the ticket, and where the truck was while the driver was clocked in. Drivers also work where cell service drops.

What the driver records

The driver clocks in, photographs the ticket, and the app tracks location only while that driver is clocked in. Clock events and GPS points wait in an offline queue and send when a connection is available. The driver's company can see that data.

In the beta

These features are in the current beta.

Clock in and clock out

Times are filled in automatically. Drivers cannot change them.

Ticket photos

Drivers upload a photo of a ticket and can open it full screen.

Submitted tickets

Submitted tickets are listed in the app.

GPS while clocked in

Location is tracked while a driver is clocked in, including background tracking.

Offline queue

Clock events and GPS points stay on the device and send when a connection is available.

Missing-ticket alerts

The app includes alerts for missing tickets.

Drivers also sign in, use settings, and send feedback from inside the app. An owner live map, invoicing and reports, and admin screens are coming soon.

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Driver clocks in

    The time is filled in automatically. Drivers cannot change it.

  2. Step 2

    Driver snaps tickets

    A photo of the ticket can be opened full screen. Submitted tickets are listed in the app.

  3. Step 3

    Owner sees the data

    Clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was clocked in are visible to the driver's company.

Driver and owner steps

Built for construction hauling

RouteRock is for owners and dispatchers who run dump-truck work. Jerjuan Edwards is the founder. The first company using it is LoadMax Logistics in Charlotte, North Carolina. LoadMax hauls in North Carolina and South Carolina.

The app is in beta and is planned so more than one company can use it. The name in the app stores is RouteRock.
