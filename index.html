Who uses RouteRock?

Drivers, owners, and dispatchers at dump-truck fleets. RouteRock is in early access.

Drivers

  • Clock in and out. Times fill in. Drivers cannot edit them.
  • Photograph the ticket and open it full screen.
  • Clock events and GPS points queue offline. Ticket photos do not.

Owners and dispatchers

  • Owners and dispatchers see clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was on shift.

Company portal

  • Companies manage the truck roster and sub-haulers on the web.
  • The portal is at app.routerockhub.com.

How does a haul get on record?

  1. Driver time tracking

    Clock-in and clock-out times fill in on their own. Drivers cannot edit them.

  2. Digital load tickets

    The driver photographs the ticket and can open it full screen. Submitted tickets stay in a list.

  3. GPS while clocked in

    GPS runs only while the driver is clocked in. The company can see that location, and it stops at clock-out.

Questions fleet owners ask

What is construction hauling software?

Construction hauling software covers dump-truck work moving dirt, aggregate, and asphalt: load tickets, driver time, and what the company can see. The category often runs from dispatch through billing. RouteRock's early access covers tickets, time, and GPS while clocked in. See construction hauling software.

What is the best app for tracking dump truck tickets?

RouteRock is a dump truck ticket app. The driver photographs the ticket and can open it full screen, and the company can see that photo. This site does not rank or score other apps.

How do haulers stop losing paper tickets?

The driver photographs the ticket and can open it full screen. Submitted tickets stay in a list, and missing ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a photo.

Can drivers edit their clock times?

No. Clock-in and clock-out times fill in on their own. Drivers cannot change them.

Does it work without cell signal?

Clock events and GPS points stay in an offline queue on the phone and send when a connection is available. Ticket photos are not held in that queue.

When does GPS run?

GPS runs only while the driver is clocked in, in the foreground and in the background. The company can see that location.

Who sees the data?

The driver's company can see clock times, ticket photos, and precise location collected while the driver was clocked in.

Is RouteRock dump truck dispatch software?

Not yet. Dispatch and a live fleet map, ticket OCR, geofencing and route history, invoicing and accounts receivable, payroll and settlements, profit and operations reporting, and a notification center are coming soon. Arrival times are not included. Today the company sees the clock, the ticket photo, and location from while the driver was on shift.

Which phones does it support?

RouteRock runs on iPhone and Android during early access. It is not a public App Store or Google Play release.
