RouteRock

A mobile operations app for construction hauling companies.

Drivers sign in, clock in and out, photograph tickets, and share location while they are clocked in.

What it is

RouteRock is for owners and dispatchers who run dump-truck work. Drivers use it on the job. Jerjuan Edwards is the founder.

The first company using it is LoadMax Logistics in Charlotte, North Carolina. LoadMax hauls in North Carolina and South Carolina.

The app is in beta and is planned so more than one company can use it. The name in the app stores is RouteRock.

In the beta

These features are in the current beta.

  • Driver login

    Drivers sign in to an account.

  • Clock in and clock out

    Times are filled in automatically. Drivers cannot change them.

  • Ticket photos

    Drivers upload a photo of a ticket and can open it full screen.

  • Submitted tickets

    Submitted tickets are listed in the app.

  • Offline queue

    Clock events and GPS points stay in a queue on the device and send when a connection is available.

  • Missing-ticket alerts

    The app includes alerts for missing tickets.

  • Settings

    Settings are in the app.

  • In-app feedback

    Feedback can be sent from inside the app.

  • GPS while clocked in

    The app tracks location while a driver is clocked in, including background tracking.
