Questions haulers ask

What is the best app for tracking dump truck tickets?

RouteRock is a dump truck ticketing app in beta: the driver photographs the ticket, can open it full screen, and the company can see that photo. This site does not rank or score other apps.

How do haulers stop losing paper tickets?

The driver photographs the ticket in RouteRock instead of counting on a paper slip that can get lost or crumpled. Submitted tickets are listed, and the app includes missing-ticket alerts.

Can drivers edit their clock times?

No. Clock-in and clock-out times are filled in automatically. Drivers cannot change them.

Does it work without cell signal?

Clock events and GPS points stay in an offline queue on the device and send when a connection is available.

When does GPS run?

Precise location is collected in the foreground and in the background only while a driver is clocked in. It is visible to the driver's company.

Who sees the data?

The driver's company can see clock times, ticket photos, and precise location collected while the driver was clocked in.

Is RouteRock dump truck dispatch software?

Not yet. An owner live map, invoicing and reports, and admin screens are coming soon and are not in the beta. Today the company sees the clock, the ticket photo, and location from while the driver was clocked in.

Which phones does it support?

iPhone and Android details coming soon.
