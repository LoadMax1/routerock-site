What is construction hauling software?

Construction hauling software is the system a dump-truck fleet uses to run dirt, aggregate, and asphalt hauls. A driver leaves with a load ticket. The office needs that ticket and the hours. The company needs to see which truck was on the shift.

The category usually runs from dispatch through billing. RouteRock is in early access. The focus today is the ticket photo, driver time, and GPS while the driver is clocked in. Dispatch, invoicing, and the rest of that path are coming soon. No launch date is listed.

Available now

What does RouteRock do today?

Available now: driver time tracking, digital load tickets, ticket history, missing-ticket alerts, GPS while clocked in, an offline queue for clock and GPS, company visibility, and a company portal for trucks and sub-haulers. Clock events and GPS points queue and sync when online. Ticket photos are not queued offline.

Driver time tracking

Clock in and out with auto-captured times. Drivers cannot edit them.

Digital load tickets

Photograph or pick a dump ticket from the camera or gallery while on shift.

Ticket history

The company and the driver see submitted ticket photos and can open them full screen.

GPS while clocked in

Location runs only during the shift and stops at clock-out.

Offline queue for clock and GPS

Clock events and GPS points queue and sync when online. Ticket photos are not queued offline.

Missing-ticket alerts

Flags a haul that still needs a ticket photo after the threshold.

Company visibility

Owners and dispatchers see clock times, ticket photos, and location from while the driver was on shift.

Company portal — trucks & sub-haulers

Companies manage the truck roster and sub-haulers at app.routerockhub.com.

The same items are on the dump truck ticketing app page.

How do haulers stop losing paper tickets?

The driver photographs the ticket and can open it full screen. Submitted tickets stay in a list, and missing ticket alerts flag a haul that still needs a photo.

Does it work without cell signal?

Clock events and GPS points stay in an offline queue on the phone and send when a connection is available. Ticket photos are not held in that queue.

Can drivers edit their clock times?

No. Clock-in and clock-out times fill in on their own. Drivers cannot change them.

Which phones does it support?

RouteRock runs on iPhone and Android during early access. It is not a public App Store or Google Play release.
